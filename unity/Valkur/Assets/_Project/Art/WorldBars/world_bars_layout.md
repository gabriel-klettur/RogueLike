# World bar skin — sheet layout

Generated by `Valkur > UI > Export World Bar Skin Template`. Do not edit by hand:
every number below comes from `WorldBarSheetLayout`, which is also what the runtime
generator draws into and what the importer slices.

Sheet: **64 x 32**, PPU **16**, Point filter, Uncompressed, sprite mode Multiple.

One pixel of this PNG is one texel of the world grid, which is one screen pixel at
the snapped camera. There is no room for antialiasing anywhere on the sheet.

## Painting rules

- **Greyscale only.** `SpriteRenderer.color` MULTIPLIES, and the colour comes from
  `WorldBarStyle.asset`. A red pixel tinted green renders black.
- **Leave headroom on the fill.** Keep its body around 217 and the RIGHT-most column
  at 255: that column is the leading edge, the part of the bar the eye tracks while it
  moves, and painting everything at 255 leaves no way to make it brighter.
- **The plate is mostly 255**, with its TOP row near 140 (the recess shadow) and its end
  columns near 205. It is tinted with a dark colour, so what you paint light comes out
  dark and what you paint dark comes out black.
- **Frames: white, opaque, with the four corner texels at alpha 0.** That hole is the
  chamfer, and it is the whole difference between a frame and a rectangle.
- **Height is not free** for `plate_*` and `fill_*`: they are not sliced vertically, so
  they stretch whole and art painted at another height gets resampled. **Width is free**
  above the border sum — only the border columns survive, the middle is stretched.
- Icons are white on transparent, in `StatusEffectKind` order:
  - `icon_0` = Burn
  - `icon_1` = Poison
  - `icon_2` = Stun
  - `icon_3` = Freeze
  - `icon_4` = Slow
  - `icon_5` = Root
  - `icon_6` = Vulnerable
  - `icon_7` = Marked

## After painting

Run `Valkur > UI > Import World Bar Skin`. Each slot is resolved on its own, so a
half-painted sheet is a legitimate state: painted pieces switch over, the rest stay on
the generated art. `Valkur > UI > Clear World Bar Skin` puts everything back.

A piece whose PPU, size or border disagrees with this table is refused at runtime with
one warning and falls back to the generated one — which is what catches the sheet being
imported under a folder the asset postprocessor treats as screen UI.

Current geometry: health row 4 texels, resource row 3, pip 5, icon 5.
Changing any of those in the style asset invalidates the art painted for the old ones.
